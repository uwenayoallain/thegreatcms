The desk is a door laid across two trestles, which is the best desk I have ever owned. It cost almost nothing and it is large enough to be untidy on.

## Rituals, not routines

A routine is something you follow. A ritual is something that tells you where you are. Mine are small: the kettle, the window opened a hand's width, yesterday's last sentence read aloud before I write today's first.

None of these makes the writing better. All of them make it more likely that I sit down.

## The company of other people's work

I keep one book open on the left side of the desk, never the one I am reading, always one I have finished. It is there as company. When a paragraph will not come, I read a page of somebody who solved a harder problem, and I go back to mine a little braver.
