The bus from Kigali to Huye takes three hours if nothing goes wrong, and something always goes wrong in a way that makes the trip better.

## The school

The classroom where I taught has a new roof and the same desks. Somebody has painted over the crack in the wall that I used to point at when explaining metaphor (_this is a crack, and it is also the way the light gets in_). I was sorry to see it go and glad they had fixed it.

## The students

Three of my former students found me at the market. One is a nurse, one runs a shop, one is a teacher at the school where I taught. She told me she still uses my crack-in-the-wall lesson, and that she points at the place where it used to be.

That is the thing about a place you have worked in for a long time: it keeps the shape of what you did there, long after the thing itself is painted over.
