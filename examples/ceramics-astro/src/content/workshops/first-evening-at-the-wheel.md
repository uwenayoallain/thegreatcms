Everyone starts in the same place: a lump of clay that will not sit still. By the end of the evening you will have centred it, opened it and pulled up a wall, twice.

## What to bring

Clothes that can get muddy, and short fingernails. We have aprons, tools and tea.

## After the class

Pick your favourite cup. We trim it, glaze it in the colour you choose, and fire it. It is ready to collect two weeks later.
