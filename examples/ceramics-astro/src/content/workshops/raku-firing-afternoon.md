Raku is fired fast and finished outside. Pieces come out of the kiln glowing at around 900 degrees and go straight into a bin of sawdust, where the smoke turns bare clay black and crackles the glaze.

## Good to know

We work in the courtyard, so wear cotton and closed shoes. Bring a bisque piece you made with us, or glaze one of ours. Everything you fire goes home with you the same day.
