---
version: "2.4"
title: Focus blocks that follow you across time zones
date: 2026-10-20
kind: feature
---

Travel no longer scrambles your week. A focus block is now set in **your** hours, not the calendar's, so a morning block stays a morning block when you land in Nairobi.

- Blocks keep their local time when you change time zone, and say so.
- A trip in your calendar can pause focus hours for its length.
